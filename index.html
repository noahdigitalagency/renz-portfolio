CASE 01 Reducing Cost Per Result. Without Increasing Budget.
01

The Challenge

A coaching business wanted to generate more registrations while maintaining profitability. Despite consistent advertising spend, the average Cost Per Result continued to rise, making it increasingly difficult to scale.

Rather than increasing the advertising budget, I redesigned the campaign structure, refined audience targeting, introduced continuous creative testing, and optimized budget allocation using performance data.
