CASE 02 GENERATING REVENUE THROUGH AUTOMATION.
01

The Challenge

A webinar-based sales process relied heavily on manual follow-up, making it difficult to consistently convert attendees into opportunities.

After every webinar, attendance data had to be reviewed, contacts segmented, and follow-up messages sent manually. This created unnecessary operational work and increased the risk of missed revenue opportunities.

Rather than adding more people to manage the process, I designed an automated system that connected webinar attendance, CRM updates, SMS, email, and follow-up actions into one workflow.

The Solution

  • Automated Audience Segmentation
